## 5. Communication Platforms

Messenger is the team's primary communication channel. Viber is the secondary channel. Email is reserved for formal communications.

## 6. Team Policies

In addition to the minimum standards in Article III of the Class Project Team Constitution, Team 404 adopts the following policies:

- Acknowledge messages within 24 hours.
- Attend meetings unless advance notice is provided.
- Track assignments and violations in Notion with clear ownership and due dates.
- Submit work incrementally rather than concentrating work near deadlines.
- Address feedback to the work and deliver it respectfully.
- Communicate blockers and changed deadlines as soon as they are known.

## 7. Consequences for Missed Commitments

The team applies the following graduated response, consistent with Article IV of the Class Project Team Constitution:

1. **First instance:** The Group Leader issues an informal reminder and records it.
2. **Second instance:** The member receives a written warning supported by documented evidence and shared with the team.
3. **Third or final instance:** The team compiles evidence and refers the matter to the instructor. Removal requires instructor review and remains subject to the instructor's final decision.

A member may return to good standing by completing outstanding deliverables and demonstrating consistent participation over an agreed period.

## 8. Dispute Resolution

1. Members shall first attempt to resolve disagreements directly and respectfully.
2. If unresolved, the Group Leader shall facilitate a discussion and document the matter.
3. If still unresolved, the matter shall be referred to the instructor with supporting documentation.
